SitStudio Support

Need help with SitStudio? Whether you're having trouble signing in, editing a video, exporting a project, or managing your account, we're here to help.

Common Support Topics

Account and Sign-In

Having trouble signing in with Google or your email and password? Contact support and describe any error messages you're seeing.

Video Editing and Exports

If you're experiencing problems importing videos, creating trajectory paths, applying effects, or exporting your finished video, please include details about what happened.

Subscriptions and Billing

SitStudio subscriptions purchased through Google Play are managed through your Google Play account.

Manage Google Play Subscriptions

For questions about SitStudio subscription features or account access, contact our support team.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly inside SitStudio under Account → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by contacting support.

View Account Deletion Instructions

Help Us Troubleshoot

When contacting support, including the following information can help us investigate your issue:

  • Device model (for example, Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra)
  • Android version
  • SitStudio app version, if known
  • Description of the issue and steps to reproduce it
  • Screenshot or screen recording, if helpful

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, payment card information, or other sensitive credentials.
